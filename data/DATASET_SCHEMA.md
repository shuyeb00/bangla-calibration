## Label encodings

- `offensive`: 0 non-offensive, 1 offensive. Code-mixed Chaos: 1 toxic. MixTox-SA: 1 toxic, 0 non-toxic or neutral.
- `sentiment`: 0 neutral, 1 positive, 2 negative.
