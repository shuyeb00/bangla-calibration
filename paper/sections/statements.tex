\section*{Broader Impact Statement}
The study concerns offensive-language detection, where miscalibrated decisions have uneven costs: missed offensive posts
harm their targets, and false flags silence speakers of under-served varieties. Our results show that a moderation
model can miss most offensive posts in a new variety while remaining confident, and that a popular label-free fix can
make matters worse on targets with few offensive posts. The triage procedure is meant to make such failures visible
and to require a small labelled check before deployment, but its guarantee on missed posts is paid for in false flags, which
fall on the same speakers. The datasets are used under their published terms; texts whose terms do not allow it are not
redistributed. Offensive examples are not reproduced in the paper.
