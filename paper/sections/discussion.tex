\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What practitioners should do.}
Detect shift with a domain classifier, not with the model's confidence. After a detected shift, correct the class bias
rather than only the temperature. Without labels, batch calibration is appropriate only when the target classes are
believed to be roughly balanced; otherwise about 50 labels estimate the prior or fit MAP BCTS. The same 50 labels also
test whether the ranking survived, choose between the fine-tuned encoder and an LLM, and certify coverage and the
false-negative rate. Fine-tuning on the target becomes the better use of labels only at a few hundred labels.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Scope.} Two classification tasks; the main grid is Bangla, the cross-source replication uses three
  architectures per source; encoders up to 560M parameters and LLMs up to 9B parameters. Generative tasks and larger
  or proprietary models are not covered.
  \item \emph{Regime thresholds} were set after inspecting the results; the regime names and the reading rule were
  fixed in advance, and a leave-source-out test shows that a label-free threshold does not transfer.
  \item \emph{Data.} The Sylheti set is small (401 items) and was translated and label-checked by the authors; a blind
  re-annotation reproduced the source labels only at the level of the source dataset's own subjectivity. OffMix-3L
  carries strong writing-style artifacts. The Spanglish calibration and test splits come from different releases, so
  their exchangeability is not guaranteed.
  \item \emph{Statistics.} Many results are descriptive means over runs; formal tests with multiplicity control are
  reported for pre-defined families. Five-versus-five designs cannot reach significance after correction in large
  families and are reported descriptively.
  \item \emph{Guarantees} are marginal over the target calibration and test items and assume exchangeability within the
  target; when the same items decide whether to deploy, the coverage bound holds jointly with the deployment event.
  \item \emph{Contamination.} No item-level memorisation was detected, but exposure of the LLMs to the public test sets
  cannot be excluded.
\end{itemize}
