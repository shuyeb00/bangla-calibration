\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Across dialects, scripts, code-mixing and languages, and for source tasks in three languages, the common failure of
fine-tuned text classifiers under language-variety shift is a misplaced decision threshold, not a wrong temperature.
Temperature scaling cannot move the threshold however many labels it is fitted on; a bias correction can, provided its
assumption about the class prior is checked with a few labels. A collapse of the ranking is rare, arises here from a
mismatch of label definitions, and no post-hoc map repairs it.
