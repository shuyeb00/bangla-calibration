\section{Label-Efficient Shift Triage}
\label{sec:shift-triage}

The regimes call for different actions: nothing, a temperature, a bias correction, or a different system. We turn this
into a procedure with stated guarantees. Proofs are in Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix-proofs}.

\begin{proposition}[No post-hoc map repairs an uninformative score]
\label{prop:impossibility}
Let $S = s(X)$ be the score of a fixed model and $g$ any measurable map from scores to the probability simplex. Then
the target log-loss satisfies $\mathbb{E}_Q[-\log g(S)_Y] \ge H_Q(Y \mid S) = H_Q(Y) - I_Q(Y; S)$. If $Y$ and $S$ are
independent under $Q$, no post-hoc map beats the constant predictor $\pi_Q$, and for any classifier $h(S)$ and any
acceptance event $A \in \sigma(S)$ the selective error is at least $1 - \max_k \pi_Q(k)$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Shrinkage versus maximum likelihood]
\label{prop:shrinkage}
In a locally quadratic approximation with per-item Fisher information $F = \tau^{-2}$ and prior variance $\sigma^2$,
the MAP estimate is the convex combination $w_n \hat\theta_n + (1 - w_n)\theta_P$ with
$w_n = n\sigma^2 / (n\sigma^2 + \tau^2)$, and it has lower mean squared error than the maximum-likelihood estimate if
and only if $\delta^2 < 2\sigma^2 + \tau^2/n$, where $\delta$ is the distance between the target optimum and the
source estimate.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Ranking-survival test]
\label{prop:survival}
For a score that is not fitted on the $n$ labelled target items, the Monte Carlo permutation p-value of the AUROC
(with the add-one correction) is valid for the null of no association between score and label at every $n$; with
$m = \min(n_1, n_0)$ labelled items per class, $\Pr(A \ge \hat A - \sqrt{\log(1/\delta)/(2m)}) \ge 1 - \delta$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Coverage and risk control after selection]
\label{prop:selection}
Let $M$ candidate systems have fixed nonconformity scores. If each conformal set is calibrated on the same $n$ items at
level $\alpha/M$, the set of any data-dependent choice covers with probability at least $1 - \alpha$; the same holds
for conformal risk control of a loss in $[0,1]$. For the false-negative rate, calibrated on the positive items only, a
non-trivial threshold needs at least $M/\alpha - 1$ labelled positives. If the choice and the calibration use disjoint
parts of the labelled items, the guarantees hold at level $\alpha$ conditionally on the choice.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Procedure.}
(1) Detect shift without labels with a character n-gram domain classifier; if a shift is detected, request $n$ target
labels. (2) Test each candidate system (the encoder and an LLM) for ranking survival (Proposition~\ref{prop:survival},
Holm over candidates at $\delta = 0.05$). (3) If no candidate survives, abstain: by
Proposition~\ref{prop:impossibility} no recalibration, conformal set or confidence threshold of these scores does
better than ignoring the input. (4) Otherwise choose the survivor with the lowest log-loss after MAP BCTS, recalibrate
it with MAP BCTS, and certify conformal sets and the FNR threshold either on all $n$ items at level $\alpha/M$
(\emph{Bonferroni}) or on a held-out half at level $\alpha$ (\emph{split}).

\paragraph{Evaluation.}
We evaluate the procedure on every shifted condition, every converged encoder run paired with each general LLM, and
20 resamples of $n \in \{25, 50, 100\}$ target labels, against always using the encoder, always using the LLM, a router
that reuses the same labels without correction for selection, and an oracle that picks the better system on the test
split (Table~\ref{tab:shift-triage-policies}). With $n = 50$ the Bonferroni variant reaches macro-F1 0.794 on the
original shifted conditions against 0.796 for the oracle, 0.788 for the reuse router, 0.715 for always-LLM and 0.658 for
always-encoder, with coverage 0.962 and a share of deployments whose test coverage falls below 0.85 of 0.006 (reuse
router 0.124, always-encoder 0.472). On the held-out natural code-mixed offensive targets it reaches 0.787 (oracle
0.792) with that share at 0.004. With $n = 25$ and about twelve positives, the Bonferroni FNR threshold is vacuous and
flags every item, as Proposition~\ref{prop:selection} predicts; the split variant pays for its conditional guarantee
with abstention (up to 0.34 of resamples on the natural targets at $n = 25$). The guarantees therefore need about 50
labelled target items in this design, which we report as the procedure's label requirement.
