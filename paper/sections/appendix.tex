\section{Proofs}
\label{sec:appendix-proofs}

\paragraph{Proposition~\ref{prop:impossibility}.}
Conditionally on $S = s$, the expected log-loss of $q = g(s)$ is the cross-entropy
$\mathbb{E}[-\log q_Y \mid S = s] = H(Q(\cdot \mid s)) + \mathrm{KL}(Q(\cdot \mid s) \,\|\, q) \ge H(Q(\cdot \mid s))$,
with equality if and only if $q = Q(\cdot \mid s)$. Taking expectations over $S$ gives the bound. Under independence
$Q(\cdot \mid s) = \pi_Q$ for almost every $s$, so the bound equals $H_Q(Y)$ and is attained by the constant $\pi_Q$.
For the selective error, independence gives $Q(Y = h(S) \mid S) = \pi_Q(h(S)) \le \max_k \pi_Q(k)$; conditioning on
$A \in \sigma(S)$ preserves the bound. $\square$

\paragraph{Proposition~\ref{prop:shrinkage}.}
With $n\hat L_n(\theta) \approx \mathrm{const} + \tfrac{n}{2}(\theta - \hat\theta_n)^\top F (\theta - \hat\theta_n)$, the
stationarity condition of the MAP objective is $nF(\theta - \hat\theta_n) + \Sigma^{-1}(\theta - \theta_P) = 0$, which
gives $\hat\theta_{\mathrm{MAP}} = W_n\hat\theta_n + (I - W_n)\theta_P$ with $W_n = (nF + \Sigma^{-1})^{-1}nF$. In the
scalar case $\hat\theta_{\mathrm{MAP}} - \theta^* = w_n(\hat\theta_n - \theta^*) - (1 - w_n)\delta$, so with
$\mathbb{E}[\hat\theta_n - \theta^*] \approx 0$ and $\operatorname{Var}\hat\theta_n \approx \tau^2/n$,
$\mathrm{MSE}(\hat\theta_{\mathrm{MAP}}) = (1 - w_n)^2\delta^2 + w_n^2\tau^2/n$. This is below $\tau^2/n$ if and only if
$\delta^2 < \frac{\tau^2}{n}\frac{1 + w_n}{1 - w_n} = \frac{\tau^2}{n} + 2\sigma^2$. The source estimate $\theta_P$ is
treated as fixed, and the prior variance is a cross-task plug-in rather than an estimate from the target population.
$\square$

\paragraph{Proposition~\ref{prop:survival}.}
Under exchangeability and independence of score and label, the joint law of the scores given the labels is invariant
under permutations, so the observed AUROC and its permuted copies are exchangeable and the add-one Monte Carlo p-value
is valid \citep{phipson2010permutation}. The AUROC is a two-sample U-statistic with kernel in $[0, 1]$; Hoeffding's
inequality for U-statistics \citep{hoeffding1963} gives $\Pr(\hat A - A \ge t) \le \exp(-2mt^2)$, and the bound
follows. It also holds for sampling without replacement from a finite calibration pool, relative to the pool AUROC.
For three classes we use only the permutation test, applied to the macro one-vs-rest AUROC. $\square$

\paragraph{Proposition~\ref{prop:selection}.}
Each set calibrated at level $\alpha/M$ on exchangeable items with a fixed score covers with probability at least
$1 - \alpha/M$; the conformal quantile is finite when $\lceil (n+1)(1 - \alpha/M) \rceil \le n$, i.e.\
$n \ge M/\alpha - 1$. For any choice $\hat\jmath$, $\{Y_{n+1} \notin C_{\hat\jmath}\} \subseteq \bigcup_j \{Y_{n+1}
\notin C_j\}$, and the union bound gives miscoverage at most $\alpha$. For risk control with losses in $[0, 1]$,
$L_{\hat\jmath} \le \sum_j L_j$ pointwise, so $\mathbb{E}[L_{\hat\jmath}] \le M \cdot \alpha/M$. For the FNR the
threshold is the $\lfloor (\alpha/M)(n_1 + 1) - 1\rfloor$-th smallest positive score, which exists only if
$n_1 \ge M/\alpha - 1$. With sample splitting the chosen candidate is independent of the calibration part and the
single-candidate guarantee applies conditionally on the choice. When the same items also decide whether to deploy at
all, the bound applies to the joint event of deploying and missing, so conditionally on deployment it is
$\alpha / \Pr(\text{deploy})$. $\square$
